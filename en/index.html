About FOI

Founded on 20 May 2015, Foodbank of Indonesia (FOI) is a non-profit social organisation under the Yayasan Lumbung Pangan Indonesia foundation. FOI’s mission is to fight hunger among vulnerable groups and build productive communities through a community-based spirit of mutual cooperation.

Programs

  • Sayap Dari Ibu

    Together with posyandu, FOI helps mothers support their child’s development during the First 1000 Days of Life through community empowerment and education on food, nutrition, and parenting.

    View Program ›

  • Mentari Bangsaku

    A community-based school feeding program that brings good food closer to children at school so they can learn and stay active to become the nation’s future bright lights.

    View Program ›

  • Dapur Pangan FOI

    A community-based empowerment initiative for women and mothers to fight hunger among children, the elderly, and vulnerable communities through local food and spice processing in a warm, family kitchen spirit.

    View Program ›

  • Community Food Garden

    KEPAK promotes natural farming practices to strengthen the food resilience of families and communities, through food gardens in urban areas and farmer mentoring in rural areas.

    View Program ›
