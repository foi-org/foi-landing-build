Careers

At Foodbank of Indonesia, we believe every individual has a role in creating better food access. Together with a collaborative, socially driven team, we open space for talents who want to grow while contributing to the community.

We welcome talents with a social spirit, a desire to develop, and a readiness to create real impact through meaningful work.

Why FOI

Why Join Us?

  • Collaborative Environment

    We work together in an open, supportive, and mutually respectful atmosphere.

  • Impact for the Community

    Every piece of work directly contributes to supporting our humanitarian mission and better food access.

  • Room to Grow

    We offer opportunities to learn, develop, and explore your potential through diverse work experiences and social programs.

  • Empathy-Based Work Culture

    We put care, integrity, and the spirit of mutual cooperation at the heart of every step.

Candidate Profile

Who We're Looking For

We're looking for individuals who:

  • Have a spirit of collaboration and social awareness
  • Are responsible and communicative
  • Are adaptive and eager to keep learning
  • Can work both individually and in a team
  • Have an interest in food, humanitarian, and community empowerment issues
